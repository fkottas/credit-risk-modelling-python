# Educational and professional-use disclaimer

This repository is educational. It is not legal, regulatory, accounting, audit, investment, or credit advice and is not a validated production model.

The examples simplify institutional requirements. In particular, the IFRS 9, CECL, Basel IRB, pricing, fairness, and agentic-AI examples require institution-specific definitions, policies, data, independent validation, approvals, controls, and jurisdictional review before any real use.

No example should be used to approve, decline, price, limit, collect from, or otherwise affect a real person. Synthetic and historical public data do not establish suitability for a modern portfolio. The governed agent examples produce recommendations for qualified human review; they do not receive authority to make customer decisions.
