## Model and AI risk

- Federal Reserve, [Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/supervisory-guidance-on-model-risk-management.htm). The project tracks the April 2026 revision rather than relying only on SR 11-7.
- NIST, [AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).
- European Union, [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 - Artificial Intelligence Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng).

The EU AI Act classifies AI used to evaluate natural persons' creditworthiness or credit score as high-risk, subject to the regulation's scope and exceptions. Prudential internal models used to calculate capital requirements are treated differently. The book therefore does not collapse origination credit scoring, IFRS 9 estimation, and IRB capital modelling into one legal category.
